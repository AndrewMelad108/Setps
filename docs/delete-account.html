Delete your SYNQ account and data

Inside the app

  1. Sign in to SYNQ.
  2. Open Account.
  3. Select Privacy and data.
  4. Select Delete my account and data.
  5. Enter your current password and confirm permanent deletion.

Request deletion without the app

Email YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL from the address registered to your SYNQ account with the subject “SYNQ account deletion request.” Do not send your password.

What is deleted

Your Firebase Authentication account, tasks, completion records, work sessions, sheets, and appointments are permanently deleted. Locally selected files are deleted through the in-app flow. No account data is retained after a verified deletion request, except where retention is legally required.
