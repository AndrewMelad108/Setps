SYNQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Before publishing: replace YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL with the developer support email used in the Google Play listing.

Data we collect

SYNQ stores your email address and Firebase user ID for authentication. Tasks, work sessions, sheets, rates, and appointments that you enter are stored in Google Firebase so they can sync to your account.

Optional analytics

Firebase Analytics is disabled unless you opt in. If enabled, it may process an app-instance identifier, masked-IP-derived approximate location, app interactions, and lifecycle events for product analytics. SYNQ does not send task names or your email as analytics event parameters.

Files and notifications

A custom completion sound selected from your device stays in SYNQ's private on-device storage. PDF and Excel reports are generated on your device and are only sent to an app or service when you choose it from the system share sheet.

Use, sharing, and security

Data is used to operate SYNQ, authenticate your account, sync your content, and—only with consent—measure app usage. We do not sell data or use it for advertising. Google Firebase processes cloud data as a service provider. Data is encrypted in transit.

Retention and deletion

We retain account data until you delete individual content or your account. In SYNQ, open Account → Privacy and data → Delete my account and data. This permanently removes the Firebase Authentication account and known cloud and local SYNQ data. You can also follow the web deletion instructions.
